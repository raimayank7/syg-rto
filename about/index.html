About RTO Codes

RTO Codes lists the transport office codes of India, state by state, and tells you which office a code such as PB-10 or MH-12 belongs to.

Where the list comes from

Every office, code and date on this site was read from the public VAHAN dashboard of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways, Government of India, on 11 November 2026. The dashboard has a State box and an Office box; our list is what the Office box shows for each state: 1665 offices and other units in 36 states and union territories.

Office names are shown as VAHAN writes them, with capital letters made easier to read. We have not added addresses, phone numbers or districts, because the dashboard does not give them and we do not guess.

What the list does not show

  • Offices that are not yet on VAHAN 4, if any.
  • A few entries that the dashboard shows without a proper code are left out of the search and named on the state page.
  • Which office a particular vehicle belongs to today. A vehicle can be moved to another office or state after it is registered.

Not a government site

This is an independent reference. It is not connected with any RTO, the Ministry or the VAHAN and Sarathi services.
