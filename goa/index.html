Goa RTO codes (GA)

Goa is a state whose vehicles carry the letters GA. The VAHAN dashboard lists 12 registering offices here, from GA-03 to GA-108. The first went on VAHAN on 3 July 2017 (Panaji RTO) and the most recent entry is 1 June 2021 (State Transport Authority, Goa).

Other units on VAHAN (three-digit codes)

VAHAN also lists 1 more units for Goa with codes of 200 and above. These are its own numbers for units such as fitness centres, unit offices and special cells. You will not normally see them on a number plate.

How to read a GA number

Take GA 03 AB 1234. GA is Goa. 03 is the office: Mapusa RTO. The letters and the last four digits are the running series of that office. More on reading number plates.

What "On VAHAN since" means

The dashboard lists the offices running on VAHAN 4, the national vehicle registration system, and shows a date beside each one. We take it to be the date that office started on VAHAN 4. It is not the date the office was opened.

Source and date. Offices, codes and dates are as listed on the public VAHAN dashboard of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site. For any work at an RTO, check with the office or on parivahan.gov.in.
