Andaman and Nicobar Islands RTO codes (AN)

Andaman and Nicobar Islands is a union territory whose vehicles carry the letters AN. The VAHAN dashboard lists 1 registering offices here, from AN-01 to AN-01. The first went on VAHAN on 15 August 2020 (Port Blair DTO) and the most recent entry is 17 February 2025 (Little Andaman).

How to read a AN number

Take AN 01 AB 1234. AN is Andaman and Nicobar Islands. 01 is the office: Port Blair DTO. The letters and the last four digits are the running series of that office. More on reading number plates.

What "On VAHAN since" means

The dashboard lists the offices running on VAHAN 4, the national vehicle registration system, and shows a date beside each one. We take it to be the date that office started on VAHAN 4. It is not the date the office was opened.

Source and date. Offices, codes and dates are as listed on the public VAHAN dashboard of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site. For any work at an RTO, check with the office or on parivahan.gov.in.

Questions people ask

What is the vehicle registration code of Andaman and Nicobar Islands?

Vehicles registered in Andaman and Nicobar Islands carry the letters AN, followed by the number of the registering office.

Which RTO is AN-01?

AN-01 is Port Blair DTO in Andaman and Nicobar Islands, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

How many RTO codes does Andaman and Nicobar Islands have?

The VAHAN dashboard lists 1 offices for Andaman and Nicobar Islands with codes from AN-01 to AN-01, and 9 other units with three-digit codes, as on 11 November 2026.
