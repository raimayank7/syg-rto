The road to a licence, in five steps

  1. Apply for a learner's licence on sarathi.parivahan.gov.in. Fee: Rs 150 for each class of vehicle, plus Rs 50 for the test.
  2. Pass the learner's test on road signs and rules.
  3. Learn to drive with a licensed driver beside you and an L plate on the vehicle. Wait at least 30 days.
  4. Book and pass the driving test. Fee: Rs 300 for each class, plus Rs 200 for the licence.
  5. Receive the driving licence by post. A digital copy also appears in DigiLocker and mParivahan.

Beware of agents and look-alike sites

The only official website for licence applications is sarathi.parivahan.gov.in, reached from parivahan.gov.in. Sites and agents that promise a licence without a test, or ask for money beyond the official fee, are not official.

Questions people ask

How do I get a driving licence in India?

First get a learner's licence by applying on sarathi.parivahan.gov.in and passing a test on signs and rules. After 30 days, book and pass the driving test. The driving licence is then issued.

How much does it cost?

The central fees for one class of vehicle are Rs 700 in all: Rs 200 at the learner stage and Rs 500 at the driving licence stage. States add their own charges.

How long does it take?

At least 30 days, because you must hold the learner's licence for 30 days before the driving test. The learner's licence itself lasts six months.

Can I apply online?

Yes. Applications, fee payment and slot booking are done on the Sarathi website. You still have to appear for the driving test in person.

Is this an official website?

No. It is an independent guide. We do not take applications or fees.
