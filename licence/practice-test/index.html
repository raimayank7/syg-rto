How this helps

The learner's test asks about road signs, traffic rules and a driver's duties. These questions cover the same ground in the same multiple choice style. They are our own questions, not the official bank, so use them to learn the subject, then try your state's mock test on Sarathi.

Tips for the real test

  • Read every option before you answer.
  • For a sign, look at the shape first: circle, triangle or rectangle.
  • If two answers look right, pick the safer action.
  • Do not rush. In most states there is enough time for every question.

What the real test covers

Rule 11 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules names four subjects: traffic signs, signals and the rules of the road; what a driver must do when the vehicle is in an accident; the care to take at an unmanned railway crossing; and the documents a driver should carry. The questions are objective type, and the rule treats 60 per cent correct answers as a pass. How many questions you get, and in which languages, is decided by each state.

A simple plan for three evenings

  1. Evening one: read the road signs page, then take the "Road signs" round here until you stop making mistakes.
  2. Evening two: read the questions and answers on rules of the road and safety. Read the reason under each answer, not only the answer.
  3. Evening three: take three mixed rounds of 15 here, then your state's mock test on Sarathi, which uses the state's own question style.

Your best score on this device is remembered in your browser only. Nothing is sent to us. If you miss the real test or do not pass, the re-test fee is Rs 50 and you book a fresh slot; see Sarathi help.
