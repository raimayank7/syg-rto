Learner's licence and driving licence fees

These are the central fees listed on the Parivahan website. For one class of vehicle they come to Rs 700 from start to finish, before state charges.

ForFee, Rs
Issue of learner's licence, for each class of vehicle150
Learner's licence test, or repeat test50
Driving test, or repeat test, for each class of vehicle300
Issue of a driving licence200
Issue of International Driving Permit1,000
Addition of another class of vehicle to a driving licence500
Endorsement or renewal of authorisation for vehicles carrying hazardous goods1,000
Renewal of driving licence200
Renewal applied for after the grace period300, plus 1,000 for each year or part of a year of delay after the grace period
Change of address or other particulars recorded in the licence200
Appeal against an order of the licensing authority500

How the fees add up

Fees are charged for each class of vehicle. A person applying for a car only pays for one class. A person applying for a motor cycle and a car pays for two.

What is not in this table

  • State charges. States add user charges, smart card fees or service fees. That is why the amount on your receipt is higher than the table.
  • Repeat tests. Each repeat of the learner's test (Rs 50) or the driving test (Rs 300 for each class) is paid again.
  • Driving school fees. These are private charges and have nothing to do with the government fee.

Pay only on the official site

Fees are paid on sarathi.parivahan.gov.in or at the RTO counter, and you get a receipt. Nobody needs to be paid extra to "get the licence made". If someone asks, it is not an official fee.

Questions people ask

What is the total fee for a learner's licence and driving licence for a car?

For one class of vehicle the central fees add up to Rs 700: Rs 150 and Rs 50 at the learner stage, and Rs 300 and Rs 200 at the driving licence stage. State user charges are extra.

What is the fee for both a motor cycle and a car?

For two classes the central fees add up to Rs 1,150: Rs 300 and Rs 50 at the learner stage, and Rs 600 and Rs 200 at the driving licence stage.

What is the driving licence renewal fee?

Rs 200. If you apply after the grace period it is Rs 300, plus Rs 1,000 for each year or part of a year of delay.

What is the fee for an International Driving Permit?

Rs 1,000.

Check before you go. These facts were read on the official Parivahan website and the Delhi Transport Department website on 11 November 2026. Rules and fees change, and states add their own charges and steps. Your state's page on sarathi.parivahan.gov.in is the final word. This is not a government site.
