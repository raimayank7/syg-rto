Jammu and Kashmir RTO codes (JK)

Jammu and Kashmir is a union territory whose vehicles carry the letters JK. The VAHAN dashboard lists 20 registering offices here, from JK-01 to JK-22. The first went on VAHAN on 29 February 2016 (Pulwama ARTO) and the most recent entry is 4 May 2021 (State Transport Authority).

Other units on VAHAN (three-digit codes)

VAHAN also lists 1 more units for Jammu and Kashmir with codes of 200 and above. These are its own numbers for units such as fitness centres, unit offices and special cells. You will not normally see them on a number plate.

How to read a JK number

Take JK 01 AB 1234. JK is Jammu and Kashmir. 01 is the office: Srinagar RTO. The letters and the last four digits are the running series of that office. More on reading number plates.

What "On VAHAN since" means

The dashboard lists the offices running on VAHAN 4, the national vehicle registration system, and shows a date beside each one. We take it to be the date that office started on VAHAN 4. It is not the date the office was opened.

Source and date. Offices, codes and dates are as listed on the public VAHAN dashboard of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site. For any work at an RTO, check with the office or on parivahan.gov.in.

Questions people ask

What is the vehicle registration code of Jammu and Kashmir?

Vehicles registered in Jammu and Kashmir carry the letters JK, followed by the number of the registering office.

Which RTO is JK-01?

JK-01 is Srinagar RTO in Jammu and Kashmir, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

Which RTO is JK-02?

JK-02 is Jammu RTO in Jammu and Kashmir, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

Which RTO is JK-03?

JK-03 is Anantnag ARTO in Jammu and Kashmir, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

How many RTO codes does Jammu and Kashmir have?

The VAHAN dashboard lists 20 offices for Jammu and Kashmir with codes from JK-01 to JK-22, and 1 other units with three-digit codes, as on 11 November 2026.
