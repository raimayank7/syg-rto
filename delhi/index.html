Delhi RTO codes (DL)

Delhi is a union territory whose vehicles carry the letters DL. The VAHAN dashboard lists 16 registering offices here, from DL-01 to DL-53. The first went on VAHAN on 1 June 2015 (New Delhi (Jam Nagar House)) and the most recent entry is 19 June 2017 (Jhuljhuli Fitness Center).

How to read a DL number

Take DL 01 AB 1234. DL is Delhi. 01 is the office: Old Delhi (Mall Road). The letters and the last four digits are the running series of that office. More on reading number plates.

What "On VAHAN since" means

The dashboard lists the offices running on VAHAN 4, the national vehicle registration system, and shows a date beside each one. We take it to be the date that office started on VAHAN 4. It is not the date the office was opened.

Source and date. Offices, codes and dates are as listed on the public VAHAN dashboard of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site. For any work at an RTO, check with the office or on parivahan.gov.in.

Questions people ask

What is the vehicle registration code of Delhi?

Vehicles registered in Delhi carry the letters DL, followed by the number of the registering office.

Which RTO is DL-01?

DL-01 is Old Delhi (Mall Road) in Delhi, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

Which RTO is DL-02?

DL-02 is New Delhi (Jam Nagar House) in Delhi, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

Which RTO is DL-03?

DL-03 is South (Lado Sarai) in Delhi, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

How many RTO codes does Delhi have?

The VAHAN dashboard lists 16 offices for Delhi with codes from DL-01 to DL-53, and 7 other units with three-digit codes, as on 11 November 2026.
