How to read a TN number

Take TN 01 AB 1234. TN is Tamil Nadu. 01 is the office: Chennai (Central) RTO. The letters and the last four digits are the running series of that office. More on reading number plates.

What "On VAHAN since" means

The dashboard lists the offices running on VAHAN 4, the national vehicle registration system, and shows a date beside each one. We take it to be the date that office started on VAHAN 4. It is not the date the office was opened.

Source and date. Offices, codes and dates are as listed on the public VAHAN dashboard of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site. For any work at an RTO, check with the office or on parivahan.gov.in.

Questions people ask

What is the vehicle registration code of Tamil Nadu?

Vehicles registered in Tamil Nadu carry the letters TN, followed by the number of the registering office.

Which RTO is TN-01?

TN-01 is Chennai (Central) RTO in Tamil Nadu, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

Which RTO is TN-02?

TN-02 is RTO Chennai (North West) in Tamil Nadu, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

Which RTO is TN-03?

TN-03 is Chennai (North-East) RTO in Tamil Nadu, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

How many RTO codes does Tamil Nadu have?

The VAHAN dashboard lists 67 offices for Tamil Nadu with codes from TN-01 to TN-99, and 81 other units with three-digit codes, as on 11 November 2026.
