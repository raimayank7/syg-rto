How to read a MP number

Take MP 04 AB 1234. MP is Madhya Pradesh. 04 is the office: Bhopal RTO. The letters and the last four digits are the running series of that office. More on reading number plates.

What "On VAHAN since" means

The dashboard lists the offices running on VAHAN 4, the national vehicle registration system, and shows a date beside each one. We take it to be the date that office started on VAHAN 4. It is not the date the office was opened.

Source and date. Offices, codes and dates are as listed on the public VAHAN dashboard of the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site. For any work at an RTO, check with the office or on parivahan.gov.in.

Questions people ask

What is the vehicle registration code of Madhya Pradesh?

Vehicles registered in Madhya Pradesh carry the letters MP, followed by the number of the registering office.

Which RTO is MP-04?

MP-04 is Bhopal RTO in Madhya Pradesh, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

Which RTO is MP-05?

MP-05 is Hosangabad DTO in Madhya Pradesh, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

Which RTO is MP-06?

MP-06 is Morena DTO in Madhya Pradesh, as listed on the VAHAN dashboard on 11 November 2026.

How many RTO codes does Madhya Pradesh have?

The VAHAN dashboard lists 52 offices for Madhya Pradesh with codes from MP-04 to MP-71, and 1 other units with three-digit codes, as on 11 November 2026.
